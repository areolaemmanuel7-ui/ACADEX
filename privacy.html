Privacy Policy

ACADEX collects account and profile details that users submit, plus content users choose to publish. Data is used to operate accounts, display community content, moderate submissions, and maintain platform security.

Do not publish passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information. ACADEX uses Supabase for authentication and database services. External book catalogues are queried directly from the browser; their own privacy terms apply.

Users may request correction or deletion of their account information by contacting the platform operator. Content may remain in backups or records where required for security, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

This is a starter policy and should be reviewed for the actual operator, applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements, retention periods, and contact details before public launch.
